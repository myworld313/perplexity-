# Exam Cost and IAPP Membership

## Core exam cost

The standard CIPP/US exam fee is USD 550. An exam purchase generally must be scheduled and completed within one year or the exam fee may be forfeited. Verify current IAPP pricing and terms before purchase.

## Is membership required?

IAPP membership is not required to register for or take CIPP/US. After passing, the credential is maintained through one of two routes:

| Route | Typical cost | What it provides |
|---|---:|---|
| IAPP membership | USD 295 per year | Certification maintenance as a member benefit, plus resources, discounts, and community access |
| Certification Maintenance Fee | USD 250 per certification term | Credential maintenance without a membership |

The passed credential is not considered active until the applicable membership or Certification Maintenance Fee requirement has been met.

## Optional preparation budget

| Item | Typical cost | Required? |
|---|---:|---|
| Official textbook | About USD 55 | No |
| Official practice exam | About USD 55 | No |
| Official online training | About USD 1,195 | No |
| Third-party training | Varies | No |

## Decision guide

Membership is generally more valuable for a professional actively building a privacy career and using IAPP education, web conferences, KnowledgeNet events, resources, networking, and member discounts. The Certification Maintenance Fee can suit someone who needs the active credential but has another reliable source of qualifying CPE.

## Example self-study budget

A self-study first year could include USD 550 for the exam, USD 55 for the official textbook, USD 55 for practice questions, plus either USD 295 membership or the applicable Certification Maintenance Fee.
