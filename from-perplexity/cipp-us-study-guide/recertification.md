# CIPP/US Recertification and Maintenance

## Maintenance model

CIPP/US normally does not require a routine exam retake. Credential holders maintain the certification by meeting Continuing Privacy Education (CPE) requirements and maintaining IAPP membership or paying the Certification Maintenance Fee.

## CPE requirement

Plan for 20 CPE credits during each two-year certification term for one credential. Consult the current IAPP CPE policy and MyIAPP account for the authoritative eligibility, reporting, and deadline requirements.

IAPP programs may post credit automatically. For qualifying external education, retain completion records and submit activity through IAPP's applicable CPE submission process.

## Useful CPE subjects

- Financial-services privacy, GLBA, FCRA, and consumer-data governance.
- Data mapping, retention, deletion, and privacy notices.
- Vendor risk, data-processing agreements, and third-party oversight.
- Incident response, breach notification, and cybersecurity governance.
- State privacy laws, health data, biometrics, and children's data.
- Privacy engineering, AI governance, automated decision-making, and privacy by design.

## Failure to maintain

If the CPE requirement or membership/maintenance-fee obligation is missed, IAPP can suspend the credential. Reinstatement follows IAPP's current policy.

## Tracking workflow

1. Record the certification-term start and end dates and target CPE total.
2. Save completion evidence for every eligible course, webinar, conference, presentation, or publication.
3. Check the MyIAPP transcript quarterly.
4. Submit external CPE well before the close of the two-year term.
5. Renew membership or pay the maintenance fee on time.
