## Quick facts

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Credential | Certified Information Privacy Professional/United States (CIPP/US) |
| Issuer | International Association of Privacy Professionals (IAPP) |
| Focus | U.S. private-sector privacy law and regulation |
| Exam | 90 multiple-choice questions; 2.5 hours |
| Standard exam fee | USD 550 |
| Membership | Not required to take the exam; membership or a Certification Maintenance Fee is required after passing to keep the credential active |
| Ongoing maintenance | Continuing Privacy Education requirements plus membership or the Certification Maintenance Fee |

## Purpose

CIPP/US demonstrates working knowledge of U.S. privacy laws and regulations. It is useful for work in banking, payments, lending, insurance, healthcare, advertising technology, cybersecurity, vendor management, privacy operations, and GRC.

## Important note

IAPP periodically updates the Body of Knowledge, exam blueprint, pricing, and credential-maintenance policies. Use this repository as a structured study aid and verify final requirements against IAPP's official certification, FAQ, Body of Knowledge, and CPE-policy resources before purchasing an exam or reporting CPE.
